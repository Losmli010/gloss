### Target Language and Conventions

- Write `note` and `examples` in {{target}}; do not mix the source language into the target language.
- Original text is allowed only inside parentheses or double quotes, and each parenthesis or quotation keeps a single language; code stays verbatim and appears only inside inline backticks or fenced code blocks.
- Use the punctuation of {{target}}; leave one half-width space between Chinese and Latin words or digits; do not use emoji; when the target language is Chinese, use simplified characters only.
- Mathematical notation may appear anywhere, with formulas inside $ or $$ delimiters; single-letter mathematical variables may appear directly in prose.
